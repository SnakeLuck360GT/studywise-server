Connect Studywise

  1. Make an account here (or log in).
  2. On your account page, make an API key and copy it.
  3. In Studywise, open Settings → Marketplace, paste this site's address, , and your key.

What you can do from the app

  • Browse courses and open them to see each week's topics and sections, and which model wrote them.
  • Add a week to one of your courses, or to a new one. It doesn't generate anything, so it costs no tokens.
  • Publish a week you've made, from the week's ⋯ menu. Only weeks made in Studywise can be uploaded.
  • Before you plan a week, Studywise checks whether someone has already published one made from the same slides, and asks you first.

Only publish slides and transcripts you're allowed to share.
